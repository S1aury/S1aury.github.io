## 01 — Overview

A short game about pretending to be human. Adjust your facial features at the right moments to avoid detection. Awkward expressions and timing are central to the intended humor.

| Project | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Development | One-week game jam |
| Status | Completed jam project |
| My role | Lead Designer |

## 02 — Gameplay

The interface separates the face into three controls. Each has a row of expressions and a pair of keys. The face preview shows the combination you have chosen.

## 06 — Design Focus

The design centers on a simple contrast: trying to appear normal while the separate facial features produce awkward combinations.
