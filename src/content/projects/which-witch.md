## Problem

When I joined this UCI Video Game Design Club project, work was underway but the gameplay direction was unclear. Running the potion shop needed a goal that connected orders, ingredients and progression.

I was the only Game Designer in a team of more than ten students, working over approximately one academic quarter.

## Decision



I set a concrete objective: **earn more than the neighboring witch after three in-game days.** The rival and deadline gave management choices a shared time horizon.

<div class="flow-strip"><span>Buy ingredients</span><b>→</b><span>Brew</span><b>→</b><span>Sell</span><b>→</b><span>Income & upgrades</span></div>

This is the designed management loop. Purchase and brewing screens document implemented interfaces; the complete rival, upgrade and balance systems are not confirmed as delivered.

## Implementation evidence

**I designed** the loop, economy relationships, order priorities, win condition and pacing. Other team members implemented the features.

The screenshots below document the playable foundation. They do not establish that every proposed upgrade or competitive rule was finished.

## Results & reflection

<span class="status">Partly implemented</span>

The team delivered some systems and a playable foundation on itch.io. The broader design remained partly implemented when graduating members reduced development capacity.

My contribution was giving the team a connected design direction. A concrete set of economy revisions and test observations is still needed to assess the balance work.

Full team credits, including Beiming Xu under Design, are available on the itch.io page.
